Structural intelligence

The proprietary
rolling pin hinge.

Developed in collaboration with Dr. Hard at the Design Department of the University of Applied Sciences Potsdam, Germany, this exclusive watch-bracelet-inspired pin design rethinks the temple hinge.

Its high-density fibre pin assembly opens and closes with a hydraulic-like action, replacing conventional screw construction and helping reduce screw wear over time.

Colour in dimension

PVD colour,
made vivid.

Advanced vacuum PVD coating allows colour to bond evenly to the titanium surface, creating a richer palette across the frame.

From lake blue and lime green to pink and other vibrant tones, colour becomes an integral part of the Must by Grafix expression.

Craftsmanship

Laser cutting,
triple polishing.

Supported by industrial-scale production tools, Must by Grafix pairs high-precision laser cutting with three successive polishing stages.

Frames first undergo 72 hours of initial machine polishing, followed by 48 hours of medium-grade refinement and a final 36 hours of powder polishing. The increasingly precise surface treatment enhances the quality of colour application.

Titanium studies

A language
of lightness.

Titanium lightness, silhouette and colour give each frame its distinct character.

Titanium eyewear

Thin β-titanium,
built to feel lighter.

Must by Grafix uses lightweight, durable thin β-titanium. The metal can be reduced to as little as 0.8 mm, giving each frame an even lighter presence. Frame weight can be as low as 3–5 g.
